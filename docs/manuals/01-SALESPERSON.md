# Working your leads — the salesperson's manual

*For Sarah, Sahad, and anybody else who works leads. Last checked against the
running system on 2026-10-01.*

You do not need to read this end to end. The first two sections are the whole
job; the rest is there for the day you need it.

---

## Your day, in one screen

**Growth → My leads.** Everything on it is yours — the page cannot show you
anybody else's, so you never have to filter for that.

Across the top, four figures. Each one is a button:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Assigned leads** | everything you hold |
| **Needs attention** | the follow-up date has passed |
| **Due today** | owed today |
| **Unread replies** | **they wrote last and are waiting on you** |

Work **Unread replies** first. On WhatsApp there is a 24-hour window after
somebody writes to you, and once it shuts you can only send an approved
template. A reply inside the window is a free conversation; outside it, it is
not.

### The Priority column

High / Normal / Low, worked out from the clock, not from your opinion:

- **High** — they wrote last, *or* the next action is overdue
- **Normal** — open, nothing late
- **Low** — won or lost, finished

You cannot set it. That is deliberate — it is the one column nobody can argue
with. **Temperature** (Hot / Warm / Cold) is the one you set, after talking to
somebody.

---

## The one rule: always leave a next action

Every lead should carry a date and a sentence saying what happens next. A lead
with no next action is the one that goes quiet for three weeks.

On the lead, **Next action** takes a short line — *"Call back about the corner
plot"* — and a date. The **No next action** figure on the desk counts the ones
missing it.

## Opening a lead

Click the **name**, not the row. The row's other controls are buttons, so
clicking the name is what opens the record.

The record carries:

- **Work this lead** — stage, temperature, next action, log what happened
- **Before you call** — an AI summary of what they actually wrote. It costs money
  to generate, so it only appears when somebody presses **Read this lead**
- **What they told the form** — their own answers, exactly as typed
- **Conversation** — the WhatsApp thread
- **Notes** and **Timeline**

### Moving the stage

The stages run: New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal pending → Quotation sent
→ Visit scheduled → Visited → Negotiation → Won / Lost.

Two refusals you will meet, and both are on purpose:

- **You cannot mark somebody Qualified** until the four qualifying answers are
  recorded — budget, who decides, what they want it for, and when. *"Not
  disclosed"* is a perfectly good answer; never having asked is not.
- **Lost needs a reason.** Pick one from the list.

## Reaching them

Four controls on every row:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Call** | opens your phone's dialler |
| **WhatsApp** | opens *our* recorded thread where the project has its own number, otherwise your own handset |
| **Email** | only where there is an address |
| **Follow-up** | schedules the next one |

⚠️ **Where the project has its own WhatsApp number, use the in-app thread.**
Messaging from your own handset means nothing is recorded — no thread, no
response time, no "who replied". Today only *Demo — Product Enquiries* has its
own number; the rest fall back to your handset, and that is expected.

## Finding things: More filters

**My leads → More filters** opens a panel with everything in one place:
Project, Stage, Priority, Temperature, Came from, What is owed, Form, and a date
range.

It applies when you press **Apply filters**, not as you click — so you can set
three things and pay for one reload. The button shows a number when filters are
on; **Clear all** removes them.

## Adding a lead yourself

**Add lead**, top right. A walk-in, a referral, somebody who rang.

As you type a phone number or email, it checks whether we already have that
person and tells you before you save. If they are already an open lead on that
project, it will refuse — that is the duplicate rule, and it is protecting you
from two people ringing the same person.

**You cannot choose the owner.** A lead you add is yours. Handing leads to other
people is the manager's job.

## Importing a list

**Campaign & Lead Desk → Import.** A spreadsheet of people from an expo, a
printed list, anything.

Four steps: Upload → Map columns → Validate → Review.

- Excel (`.xlsx`) and CSV both work
- Column names are matched automatically, including other people's spellings —
  `name`, `mobile`, `mail`, `message` all work
- **A bad row does not stop the file.** Rows that cannot become a lead are listed
  and skipped; the rest go in
- Only two things are actually required: **a name**, and **a phone or an email**

**Everything you import is yours.** A salesperson keeps their own list — that is
the rule, and the database enforces it.

A sheet cannot set a stage, an owner or a temperature. Every imported lead opens
at **New**.

---

## Exporting

**Export → Excel or CSV.** It gives you *your* leads, narrowed by whatever
filters are on screen. The file you get is also a valid file to import back.

## What you cannot do, and who can

| | |
|---|---|
| Hand a lead to somebody else | the sales manager |
| See a colleague's leads | the sales manager |
| Mark a number as spam | the sales manager |
| Share out unassigned leads | the sales manager |
| Open Live overview or Lead reports | the sales manager |
| Issue a website-form key | the sales manager |

If one of these is missing from your screen, it is not broken — it is not yours.

## Try this in ten minutes

1. Open **My leads**. Press **Unread replies**. Does the list narrow?
2. Open a lead by its **name**. Set a **temperature** and a **next action** with
   a date. Go back — does the row show it?
3. **More filters** → Priority **High** → Apply. Does every row say High?
4. **Add lead** with a phone number you know is already in the system. Does it
   warn you before you save?
5. **Export → CSV.** Open it. Are those your leads, and only yours?
6. Try to set **Qualified** on a lead nobody has qualified. It should refuse and
   tell you which answers are missing.

If any of those does something other than what this page says, that is worth
reporting — with the step number.
